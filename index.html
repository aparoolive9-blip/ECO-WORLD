Project Purpose

EcoWorld is a digital software framework designed to assist urban planners, ecologists, and communities in tracking ecological metrics, protecting local natural habitats, and meeting net-zero sustainability milestones.

Core Modules

Biodiversity Tracking

Monitor local flora/fauna health and protect high-conservation-value zones.

Water Management

Track rainwater harvesting, leak-detection systems, and groundwater tables.

Carbon Accounting

Measure real-time progress toward organizational net-zero targets by 2050.
